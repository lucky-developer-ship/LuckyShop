How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also check your order status by logging into your account and visiting the Orders section.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are securely processed with 256-bit SSL encryption.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout. Free shipping is included on orders over $50.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order enters processing. Contact customer service for assistance.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. Duties and taxes may apply.

What is your warranty policy?

Most products include a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranty options are available at checkout. Contact us for warranty claims.

Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase! If you're not completely satisfied, you can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

How to Return an Item

  1. Log into your account and go to Orders
  2. Select the order containing the item you want to return
  3. Click "Return Item" and select your reason
  4. Print the prepaid return shipping label
  5. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any carrier location

Refund Timeline

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. The credit will appear on your statement within 7-10 business days.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Place a new order for the correct item and return the original for a full refund. This ensures you get your replacement as quickly as possible.
